São Tomé and Príncipe citizenship by investment

A modern government program for those who value freedom of choice and long-term opportunity — offering foreign investors a path to citizenship under an official state framework. We make the process clear, transparent and comfortable.

The Destination

São Tomé and Príncipe in view

About São Tomé and Príncipe

São Tomé and Príncipe is a small democratic island nation in the Gulf of Guinea, made up of two main islands on the Equator. A Portuguese-speaking CPLP member, its economy has traditionally rested on cocoa and coffee and is now expanding into tourism, agriculture, renewable energy and the blue economy. In 2025, its entire territory joined UNESCO's World Network of Biosphere Reserves — a reflection of its exceptional biodiversity and relaxed pace of life.

Key Advantages

  • full citizenship — the same legal status as citizenship by birth
  • a fully remote process — no relocation required, before or after
  • processing time of about 3 months
  • family included in one application — spouse, children, parents
  • citizenship passed on to future generations
  • visa-free travel to 93+ countries, including South Africa, Singapore and Hong Kong
  • CPLP access — residence, work and study in Portugal, Brazil, Angola and Mozambique
  • a single investment — no further mandatory contributions

Who the Program Suits

  • entrepreneurs and business owners
  • investors
  • remote professionals
  • crypto industry professionals
  • those working across several countries
  • families planning ahead
  • anyone seeking greater international mobility

Applicants must be 18+, have a clean record, a legal source of funds, and pass due diligence.

Investment and Fees

A single investment contribution to the National Transformation Fund.

  • USD 90,000 — single applicant
  • USD 95,000 — family of 2 to 4 members
  • USD 5,000 — each additional family member
  • USD 5,000 — Submission Fee
  • USD 750 — government fee for the issuance of national documents

Paid only after Approval in Principle.

How the Process Works

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A licensed agent reviews your requirements and helps prepare documents.

  2. 02

    Document Preparation

    Documents are reviewed and prepared to the Program's official standards.

  3. 03

    Application Submission

    The application goes to the government authority responsible for the Program.

  4. 04

    Due Diligence

    A multi-layered background check protects the Program and its applicants alike.

  5. 05

    Approval in Principle

    Authorities review the application and issue their preliminary decision.

  6. 06

    Investment

    Once approved, the investment is made to the National Transformation Fund.

  7. 07

    Citizenship and Passport

    Citizenship is granted and national documents — ID and passport — are issued.

Typical Documentation

The exact list depends on your situation. Typically required:

  • proof of identity
  • a valid international passport
  • documents confirming marital/family status
  • documents confirming the source of funds, where required
  • further certificates as required by government authorities

We also translate and certify documents when needed. Your exact list comes after the consultation.

The National Transformation Fund & Legal Framework

The investment funds infrastructure, education, healthcare, tourism and environmental projects, with public reporting on where it goes. The Program is active — applicants from over 40 nationalities have already taken part — and operates under Nationality Law No. 07/2022 and Decree-Law No. 07/2025, administered by the Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU).

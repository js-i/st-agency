São Tomé and Príncipe: What to Know About the Programme

An overview of São Tomé and Príncipe and its government citizenship-by-investment programme — the country, the rationale, and what the process involves.

2 min read

São Tomé and Príncipe is a small island nation in the Gulf of Guinea, off the west coast of Africa. It is known for political stability, a favourable investment climate, a safe environment, and a clear focus on sustainable development — the qualities its citizenship-by-investment programme was designed to build on.

Why the programme exists

Like other citizenship-by-investment programmes, São Tomé and Príncipe's framework was created as a vehicle for attracting international capital to support the national economy and advance priority development projects. Foreign investors who meet the programme's legal requirements can obtain citizenship under an official state procedure.

Who tends to consider this programme

We typically see interest from:

  • entrepreneurs conducting international business
  • investors looking to diversify their long-term options
  • families planning ahead for their children's future
  • individuals who value expanded international mobility
  • those approaching second citizenship as part of a broader long-term strategy

What the process generally looks like

  • Consultation — understanding your situation and confirming programme fit
  • Preliminary assessment — a review of your circumstances against programme requirements
  • Document preparation — assembling the required documentation with guidance at each step
  • Application submission — filing through the established procedure
  • Review — assessment by the competent government authorities
  • Completion — finalisation of citizenship once the review concludes successfully

A note on remote processing

Whether procedures can be completed remotely depends on the programme's current requirements. Some stages typically can be; others may require a specific action from the applicant. We explain exactly what applies to your situation during the consultation, rather than making general claims that may not hold for every case.

If the São Tomé and Príncipe programme looks relevant to your goals, we would be glad to walk through the specifics with you.
